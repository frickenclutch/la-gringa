Weekday mornings (6–10 am, every hour) the site reads the TDG Facebook page. Posts about specials or menu changes show up here as drafts — nothing reaches guests until you add one to the board and save.

    How to connect (one time, about 10 minutes, on a computer)
    1. Go to developers.facebook.com and log in with the Facebook account that manages the TDG page. Click My Apps → Create App. If it asks what the app is for, choose Other, then Business. Name it “TDG Website”.
    2. In the new app open App settings → Basic. Copy the App ID, then click Show next to App secret and copy that too.
    3. Open Tools → Graph API Explorer. On the right, pick the “TDG Website” app, then under Permissions add pages_show_list and pages_read_engagement.
    4. Click Generate Access Token, approve, and tick the TDG page when asked. Copy the long token that appears.
    5. Paste all three below and press Connect. The site trades them for a page key that does not expire and forgets the secret.
